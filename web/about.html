About Us


Hello, and welcome to TradeTree!

This is a site built for users to trade and list services for trade! We know how hard it is on other sites to offer a trade when all the other users want is money, so we built TradeTree. On this site, you will be able to list an item or service for another item or service with other users.

Negotiating

When you find a listing you like, all you have to do is hit the "message" button under the post and send the user a message. In the chat, it is up to both users to agree on a place to meet to exchange and also what items or service are to be exchanged.

Meeting

When coming up with a place to meet, make sure it is a well lit and trusted area with plenty of people around if possible. Our main priority is making sure all users feel safe and trusted during the entire process.

Reviews

After a successful trade has been completed, you will have the option to review that user. When leaving a review, you will leave a star rating along with a short description (if desired) about how this person was. The start rating will be posted beside the users profile and will be averaged out between all starts left to that user. You may also check a box to remain anonymous when leaving the review to maintain a safe atmosphere.
